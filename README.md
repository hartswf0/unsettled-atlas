Unsettled Atlas

Synthetic places for testing how space becomes normal.

Unsettled Atlas turns spatial concepts into operable environments.

Orientation becomes terrain.
Power becomes access and friction.
History becomes substrate.
Relations become thresholds.
Description becomes operation.

A synthetic place is changed by being entered. Its instruments alter what they measure. Its boundaries reveal who maintains them. Every crossing changes both the traveler and the ground.

An unsettled place is not incomplete. Its conditions remain visible, contestable, and available for reconstruction.
