The placement was found by sliding the game's layout over six highland windows, in 30 m steps and 10° turns, and keeping the spot where real heights best match the game's sculpted ones. With that many tries a close match is expected somewhere, so it shows the circuit fits real ground, not that the game was modelled on this hill. Elevation is a 30 m surface model, so anything smaller than a house is smoothed away. The coffee rounds and queue rules come from the brief and have not been checked with anyone who lives there.
