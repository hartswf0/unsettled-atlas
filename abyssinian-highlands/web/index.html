Abyssinian Highlands

Seven windows on the Ethiopian Highlands and the Blue Nile, each 12 km square, seen by Sentinel-2 at 10 m per pixel. Yellow outlines are machine-drawn field units from the open Fields of the World map. Yosemite Valley sits at the end of the row as the yardstick for relief.

Steepest 12 km transect, against Yosemite Valley

What is measured and what is not. Imagery: Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A, one brightness stretch for all three bands so colours are not shifted. Elevation: Copernicus GLO-30. Field units: Fields of the World global predictions for 2025 (Robinson et al. 2026, CC BY 4.0), a model run on 10 m imagery. Ethiopia is outside the area where that model reports a confidence score, so every polygon here has none. A field unit is a patch the model saw as one field, not a legal or surveyed plot. Coordinates marked "given" came with the brief; "chosen" ones were picked for this atlas.
