Body of Other Bodies

Your body is detected by the camera and rendered as a population. Every small figure is an individual with its own scale, lag, mobility, preferred distance from neighbours and memory. None of them is you. Together they are.

Worldt + Bodyt  →  Encounter  →  Worldt+1 + Bodyt+1

Corporeal → embed → population → relation → aggregation → composite body

Privacy
Camera frames are processed entirely inside this page. Nothing is uploaded, recorded or stored. There is no account and no server. Close the tab and the population is gone.
Space
Body space — the camera's own coordinate field. Not geographic, not SLAM.
Murmur
Recent organisations of the population stay faintly present where the body has moved away from them. Stand still and they converge into you and disappear.
Confidence
Where the model is unsure of you, the population loosens, drifts and detaches. Occlude a limb and watch its inhabitants hesitate, then remember, then dissolve.
Habitude
The session remembers where movement has already been, and organises more tightly there. It remembers movement only. It infers nothing about you.
Hold
Press and hold on any small body to see its own history and its current relations. Release to let it go.
Controls
Tap or click the field to reveal them. Camera switch · camera visibility · murmur · reset · this panel.
Keys
C camera visibility · M murmur · F flip · R reset · I info · Esc close.
