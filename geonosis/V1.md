# GEONOSIS v1 · DIFFERENCE → INTERPRETATION → POSSIBILITY → GEOGRAPHIC CONSEQUENCE

Geonosis v1 does not define geography as a pile of layers and does not define a geosign as an object with coordinates.

Its operational question is:

> When does a spatial difference become interpretable for a situated system, alter possible action, and participate in producing subsequent geography?

## Four regimes

- `MATERIAL` — what physically exists, changes, flows or leaves traces.
- `AFFORDANCE` — what a situated actor can sense, reach, cross, use, avoid or risk.
- `INSTITUTIONAL` — what is owned, permitted, prohibited, governed, funded, serviced or officially recognized.
- `REPRESENTATIONAL` — what maps, databases, names, forecasts, claims and models say.

These are not GIS layers. They are partially coupled geographic regimes.

## Four geographic forms

Geonosis does not force the world into entities.

- `OBJECT`
- `FIELD`
- `TRAJECTORY`
- `RELATION`

Icosa addresses are spatial addresses only. They are never the ontology and they do not imply that all geographic scopes are nested.

## Eight runtime objects

1. `GEOGRAPHIC_FORM`
2. `OBSERVATION`
3. `INTERPRETATION`
4. `POSSIBILITY`
5. `ACTION`
6. `TRANSFORMATION`
7. `WORLD_MODEL`
8. `CORRESPONDENCE`

Legacy `SIGNAL / ENTITY / RELATION / STATEMENT` records remain valid evidence transport. `v1-bridge.mjs` can turn a legacy signal into a geographic form plus observation, but it does not create an interpretation merely because a record exists.

## Semiotic admission law

A candidate geosign must preserve:

1. spatial dependence,
2. discrimination by a situated system,
3. mediation or aboutness through an interpretive rule,
4. geographic consequence in orientation, access, flow, exposure, authority, trajectory or later spatial organization.

An `INTERPRETATION` must expose at least one alternative or falsifier. If a reading cannot in principle be wrong, Geonosis has not yet shown that it is more than causal propagation.

## Observation law

Every observation records an apparatus and its failure modes. Absence is never silently equivalent to zero. A later absence engine should distinguish at minimum:

`ABSENT / NOT_DETECTED / NOT_OBSERVABLE / NOT_SAMPLED / NOT_REPORTED / NOT_RECORDED / NOT_REPRESENTED / SUPPRESSED / EXPECTED_BUT_MISSING / UNKNOWN`.

## Correspondence law

`MISMATCH` is not proximity and not model intuition.

A correspondence requires an explicit expectation between two regimes and evidence on both sides. The current deterministic engine supports:

- `MATCH`
- `MISMATCH`
- `LAG`
- `CONTRADICTION`
- `UNKNOWN`

The central analytic object is the relation among regimes, especially when a representation, affordance, material condition or institutional state that normally corresponds stops corresponding.

## Geographic regime break

V1 uses this operational candidate:

> A geographic regime breaks when a declared cross-regime expectation fails while action still depends on the outdated or incompatible regime state.

This is intentionally narrower than calling every disturbance deterritorialization.

## Synthetic executable fixture

`fixtures/flooded-intersection-v1.json` is explicitly synthetic. It tests one complete circuit:

```text
MATERIAL flood depth
  ↓
CAR interpretation: crossing closes
  ↓
REPRESENTATIONAL map still says OPEN
  ↓
MISMATCH
  ↓
INSTITUTION declares CLOSED
  ↓
LAG
  ↓
REPRESENTATIONAL map updates CLOSED
  ↓
MATCH
  ↓
REORIENT
```

The fixture is a contract test, never geographic evidence.

## Next implementation boundary

The next production step is not another source family. It is to derive real V1 observations from the compiled Geonosis corpus and add a small set of explicit cross-regime rules where the expectation can be defended, for example:

- official road state ↔ observed/actor-relative traversability,
- official flood representation ↔ recurrent observed inundation,
- permitted development ↔ observed demolition/construction,
- scheduled transit ↔ actual vehicle movement,
- regulated facility ↔ measured/reported environmental condition.

Only after those work should V1 candidate differences seed Syntegration.
