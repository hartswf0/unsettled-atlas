What the Green Can't Say

Satellite greenness is precise about plants and silent about water. Here is one field in Toshka, every March from 2017 to 2025. Is it abandoned, or only bare this March? The imagery cannot say, and this page shows why.

What would settle it

More dates each year (is it planted in another season?), the well's pumping record, the water allocation, and the owner's plans. None of these are in a March satellite image.

Imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (Sentinel-2 L2A, one fixed rendering, no tint).
