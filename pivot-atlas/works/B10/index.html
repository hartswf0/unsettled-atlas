What You'd Need Before Drilling

The question: should a farm in East Oweinat add 20 more fields? Ask what evidence someone would need before acting. Some of it is in the satellite record. Much of it is not, and the gaps are shown as plainly as the facts.

Counts: Chen et al. inventory, East Oweinat tile footprint. "Gone" = circular fields recorded 2015–2024 with no field at that spot in 2025.

Imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (Sentinel-2 L2A, one fixed rendering, no tint). Field counts by year: Chen et al., Africa hyper-arid CPIS 1972–2025 (CC BY 4.0).
