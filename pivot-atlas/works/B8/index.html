Same Frame, Next Year

The same 84 km frame over Toshka, every March from 2017 to 2025. The crop, scale and rendering are identical in every panel, so only the ground changes.

Sentinel-2 L2A, one scene per March, reflectance 0–0.55, gamma 0.9 in every panel (no per-panel stretch). Black areas are outside that date's orbit swath. Counts: fields in the whole Toshka tile that year (Chen et al. inventory), not only this crop.

Imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (Sentinel-2 L2A, one fixed rendering, no tint). Field counts by year: Chen et al., Africa hyper-arid CPIS 1972–2025 (CC BY 4.0).
