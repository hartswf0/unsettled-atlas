Grid Over Ground

The same scale, two ways of dividing land. In the Texas Panhandle, pivots sit inside the square sections of a survey grid. In Toshka, fields are packed into a lattice of touching circles. The grid's geometry shapes the farm.

Both frames are 8 × 8 km of Sentinel-2 at 10 m. Texas grid: half-mile (804.7 m, quarter-section) squares, offset fitted to the field centres. It is an approximation of the survey grid, not the cadastral record. Toshka lines join neighbouring field centres.

Imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (Sentinel-2 L2A, one fixed rendering, no tint).
