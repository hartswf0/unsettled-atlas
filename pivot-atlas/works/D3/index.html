From Field to Number

Follow a chain of operations backward: from a national harvest statistic, to tonnes, to hectares, to fields, to one wedge of green. Each link names its transform. The links that would need records not held here are left empty.

Planted area = planted fraction × disc area, from March 2025 Sentinel-2 (NDVI > 0.30 by angular sector). Tonnes need a crop type and a yield per hectare for that crop, place and season. A national figure needs a census or ministry statistic. None of these are in the imagery.

Imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (Sentinel-2 L2A, one fixed rendering, no tint).
