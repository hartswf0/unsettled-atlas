Unsettled Atlas · pivot-atlas

SCION Family

Twenty-six works made from real center-pivot fields in open Sentinel-2 imagery. Each one learns a lesson from a reference work (a film, a chart, an instrument, a book) and rebuilds it from farms that exist, with every claim labelled as observed, composed, or not seen.

Each work opens as a claude.ai page; they are private to their owner until shared. "source" links to the work's folder in this repository (fragment, built page and its SCION instruction). Imagery contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data; field counts by year from Chen et al., Africa hyper-arid CPIS 1972–2025 (CC BY 4.0).
