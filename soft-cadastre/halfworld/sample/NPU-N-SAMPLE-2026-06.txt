CALL TO ORDER
Chair Whitfield called the meeting to order at 7:02 PM. Quorum present.

ITEM 1. V-26-118 VARIANCE, REAR SETBACK REDUCTION
Application V-26-118 seeks a rear setback reduction for a three-story
addition behind the old Sears building fronting the Eastside Trail.
Mr. Okafor spoke for the applicant. Ms. Delgado said the alley behind
the old Sears building already floods and the parking situation along
the Beltline is at capacity every weekend. Mr. Haynes agreed and said
cut-through traffic on our street has doubled since the deck opened.
Motion to deny carried 14-3. The variance was denied.

ITEM 2. SPEED TABLE PETITION, AIRLINE STREET
Ms. Delgado presented a petition for speed tables near Krog Street
Market. She said drivers leaving the Krog Tunnel treat our block as a
ramp, and children cross there to reach the trail. Mr. Haynes noted
the same speeding pattern across from Krog Street Market on the
market side. Petition endorsed; forwarded to ATLDOT. Approved.

ITEM 3. LIGHTING AT HISTORIC FOURTH WARD PARK
Commissioner Pratt reported that lighting along the north edge of
Historic Fourth Ward Park remains dark and residents feel unsafe
walking between Fourth Ward Park and Ponce City Market after events.
Ms. Ruiz said our sidewalk on the park side has no working fixture.
No action; carried to next month. Deferred.

ITEM 4. NOISE, EVENT VENUE NEAR THE PARK
Complaint of amplified noise near Piedmont Park during festival
load-out reaching residents along the Beltline. Mr. Okafor asked for
decibel logging. Chair Whitfield will request data from the venue.
No action taken.

ITEM 5. Z-26-041 REZONING, NORTH AVENUE FRONTAGE
Application Z-26-041 proposes five stories on the North Avenue
frontage near Ponce City Market with reduced parking minimums.
Ms. Ruiz spoke in support citing transit. Mr. Haynes opposed, citing
density and shadow on our community garden. Motion to defer for a
traffic study carried. Deferred.

ADJOURNMENT
Meeting adjourned 9:14 PM.
