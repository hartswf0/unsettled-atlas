      § 06
      Gates
      recorded at build
      
    
      Executable. gate.mjs prints what failed and which stage fixes it, and exits 1 if any failed. A gate that only says false makes the next person guess.
      
    
      
    
      Gate 2 is the ratio test: if the top five anchors cover less than a third of the corpus you do not have a world, you have a travelogue, and the fix is to grow the gazetteer before spending more compute. Gate 5 is the one that cannot be satisfied by working harder.

      § 08
      What is honestly wrong right now
      the alternative is a register that flatters itself
      
    
        
      
      • Registry I is now mixed, and that is more dangerous than fixture-only. 228 of 258 claims are acquired from a real API; 30 are invented. Every surface states the split, but a reader who skims will average them. Registry II is still entirely synthetic.
        • 
      
      • 85% of acquired notes produce nothing. 1242 of 1457 Atlanta observations and 576 of 674 in New Orleans matched no lexicon entry and are kept unresolved. That number measures the regex, not the city — and until an LLM tier reads them, the dimension histogram is a picture of the lexicon's vocabulary.
        • 
      
      • An OSM note's pin is where a contributor clicked. It is treated as an anchor, and spatial probability is lowered when the text gestures rather than names. It is still the weakest geometry in the system.
        • 
      
      • The corpus is contributor-shaped. OSM Notes over-represent people who edit maps. High note density is evidence of mapping attention, and reading it as neighbourhood condition would be the exact error this system is built to refuse.
        • 
      
      • HALFWORLD's anchor coordinates are eyeballed. Nine anchors, provisional, on one corridor. OSM footprints are the first real join.
        • 
      
      • --stub extraction is regex over alias phrases. It flows the pipeline and is blind to any vernacular the alias table lacks — which is most vernacular.
        • 
      
      • PDFs are not guaranteed readable. fetch-minutes.mjs saves them, tries pdftotext, and otherwise ledgers NEEDS-OCR. Those absences must never be read as quiet neighborhoods.
        • 
      
      • The registries do not share a place-id space. A SERES claim and a HALFWORLD territory box can be about the same corner and the system cannot tell. This is the most interesting missing join — it is where testimony and record would be forced to disagree in public.
        • 
      
      • Nobody has passed gate 5.
