HOW TO READ THE FIELD

Hard core — the resolved target geometry carried by the current atlas. When source-anchor geometry becomes available, it will remain separate.

Luminous field — inferred spatial extent. Wider fields mean lower spatial probability; brighter fields mean stronger combined support.

Hatching — positive and negative claims coexist for the same place and dimension.

Tap a field to inspect the sentence, evidence span, condition, source population, relation, and limiting ideal type. Hold anywhere to probe nearby evidence without forcing a point.

DIM and LANE filter the corpus. FIELD switches between atmospheric and schematic representations. The lower-right ring sets the minimum support admitted to the map.
